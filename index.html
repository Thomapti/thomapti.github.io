Pedestrian Safety System

Prototype

A collaborative project connecting vehicle detection to an LED warning, tested with a model vehicle.

Sensing
Magnet and Hall sensor.
Control
ESP32-controlled LED warning.
Road concept
Inductive loops for vehicle detection and speed estimation.
Testing notes

The model vehicle contained too little metal for reliable detection. A magnet and Hall sensor provided a practical way to test the warning sequence. This demonstrated the model setup; the inductive-loop road design remained a proposed approach.
